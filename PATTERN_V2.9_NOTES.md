# V2.9 Pattern simplification

Changed: Image to Pattern extraction, with center focus crop, simplified edges, removal of long horizontal/vertical borders, connected-component small detail filtering and soft embossed overlay. Added Focus and Simplify sliders.

Unchanged: other tools, 8 traditional stamps, stamp size, 10 dough colors, gacha, sending, collection, and the visual theme.

Limitations: It is not semantic object recognition. It cannot reliably isolate an intricate logo in a photograph or recognize which part the user intends. For those images, crop the source around the target symbol, and use a simple high contrast image. The new routine is intended to keep major strokes rather than copying every pixel.
