# V2.3: 3D Dough All Previews

- Dough selection: approved transparent dough art remains on flavor cards and the plate.
- Shape preview: dough texture is clipped to the selected shape, keeping the original mold outline.
- Decorate/Bake/Package: same material is clipped to the cookie under traditional stamps, icing, and toppings.
- Cookie PNG and Greeting Card export: texture data is embedded in the SVG (no external image request), so exports work offline in Safari.
- Gacha animation, poetry API, email safety lock, collections and UI design are unchanged.

Note: Textured shapes are rendered by clipping and stretching the selected 3D dough material to the mold. This preserves consistent color and texture but is not a new physically modeled 3D sculpture for each shape.
